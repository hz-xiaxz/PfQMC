## Single-Particle Representation

In determinantal QMC (and PfQMC), we work with **single-particle matrices**.
If a many-body operator is an exponential of a quadratic form:
$$ \mathcal{U} = e^{\frac{1}{2} \Psi^T A \Psi} $$
then its action on the single-particle Green's function or propagators is represented by the matrix exponential $e^A$ (or related forms depending on definitions).

### The Operator $\mathcal{S}$
$$ \mathcal{S} = \exp\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \sigma_m \psi^{(\alpha)} \psi^{(\beta)} \right) $$
Rewrite in vector form $\Psi = (\psi^{(\alpha)}, \psi^{(\beta)})^T$:
$$ \frac{\pi}{4} \sigma_m \psi^{(\alpha)} \psi^{(\beta)} = \frac{1}{2} \Psi^T \left[ \frac{\pi}{4} \sigma_m \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \right] \Psi $$

The single-particle matrix $B$ corresponding to this operator is the exponential of the inner matrix:
$$ B = \exp\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \sigma_m \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \right) $$

### Computing the Matrix $B$
Let $J = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. Note $J^2 = -I$.
$$ B = \exp\left( \frac{\pi}{4} \sigma_m J \right) = \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) I + \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) \sigma_m J $$
$$ B = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} I + \frac{\sigma_m}{\sqrt{2}} J $$
$$ B = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & \sigma_m \\ -\sigma_m & 1 \end{pmatrix} $$
